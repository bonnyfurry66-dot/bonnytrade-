Frequently asked questions

Is any of this real money?

No. Balances are virtual and cannot be withdrawn. BONNYTRADE does not take deposits.

Are prices live?

No. Every displayed price is generated by the built-in simulator in your browser. There is no live market connection.

How is a trade settled?

Rise wins if the price at expiry is above your entry price. Fall wins if it is below. An equal price returns your stake. A win pays 85% of your stake as profit.

Can I deposit or withdraw money?

No. Deposits, withdrawals, real-money trading and payment processing are not available in BONNYTRADE Demo.

Risk disclosure

Binary options and similar products can lose your entire stake and are restricted or banned in some countries. BONNYTRADE is an educational simulator. Demo results do not predict real trading results, and demo balances have no cash value.
